Welcome to the Urine Test Strip Reader Portal

This system is designed to assist healthcare professionals in analysing urine test strips using a calorimeter-based Point-of-Care Technology.

This portal provides patient registration, urine test analysis, diagnostic reports and healthcare awareness information.

System Information

Hospital: K. J. Somaiya Hospital and Research Centre

Software Version: 1.0

Current Year: 2026

Technology: HTML5

Purpose of the System

The Urine Test Strip Reader is designed to support rapid and structured urine analysis at the point of care. It can help healthcare professionals interpret various urine parameters and generate diagnostic reports.
