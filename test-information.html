Urine Test Strip Reader System

Urine Test Information

About the Urine Test

A urine test is a common diagnostic procedure used to examine different physical and chemical properties of urine. The Urine Test Strip Reader helps healthcare professionals analyse multiple urine parameters quickly.

Important Test Parameters

The system measures Glucose, Protein, Ketones, Blood, Bilirubin, Nitrite, and Leukocytes.

Healthcare Instructions

Patients should provide a clean and properly collected urine sample for accurate testing.

Healthcare professionals should follow the recommended testing procedure while handling urine test strips.

Urine Test Measurements

Glucose concentration may be represented in mg/dL

Urine pH indicates the acidity or alkalinity of the sample. The chemical formula for water is H2O

Concentration values may be expressed using units such as mg/dL, where dL represents decilitre.

The normal pH scale generally ranges from approximately 4 to 8

Medical Abbreviations

UTI refers to Urinary Tract Infection.

RBC refers to Red Blood Cells.

WBC refers to White Blood Cells.

pH indicates the acidity or alkalinity of urine.

SG represents the concentration of urine compared with water.
